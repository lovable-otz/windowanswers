How this site works

How a page here gets made

This site is paid when it refers a reader to a local company. That is an incentive to tell you what you want to hear, so the process below is built to push against it, and it runs before anything is published rather than after a complaint.

  1. 1

    Research first

    A brief is built for the page before a word of it is written. Every fact in that brief carries a source URL and a publication date. Anything that cannot be sourced is dropped at this stage rather than estimated.

  2. 2

    Write only from the brief

    The page is written from that brief and nothing else. No statistic joins a page at the writing stage because it sounded plausible, which is the failure this separation exists to prevent.

  3. 3

    Nine checks before publication

    Automated gates run on the finished page. The one that matters most extracts every number, date and named authority and matches each back to the brief. Anything unmatched comes out.

  4. 4

    Dated, then re-checked

    The review date is printed on the page. Prices move, so a figure that was right last year gets revisited rather than left to quietly go stale.

What you will never find here

Each of these is a check that fails a page and stops it publishing, not a promise we are asking you to take on trust.

  • No statistic, price or date that is not traceable to a named source
  • No reviews, ratings, star scores or testimonials written by us
  • No claimed license, premises, or years in business. We do not install windows
  • No promise of a price, a response time, an availability or an outcome

Questions about this site

Before you rely on anything here

Questions about windows are answered on the pages above, with sources. These are about how this site operates and who is paying for it.

Are you a window contractor?

No. Window Answers is an independent information site. We do not install, repair, measure, quote, inspect or warranty anything, and we are not licensed to. If you ask to be connected, your inquiry goes to independent local companies who are.

How does this site make money?

We may be paid when we refer a reader to a local company. That is the business model and we state it on every page rather than bury it. It creates an incentive to send you onward, which is exactly why the editorial rules are written down and applied before a page is published.

Where do the prices on this site come from?

Every figure is taken from a named source and carries the date it was last reviewed. Anything that cannot be sourced is left out rather than estimated. Prices appear as ranges because window replacement is priced locally, and a national range can be some way off for your market.

Will I get sales calls just for reading a page?

No. Nothing on this site asks for your phone number in order to read it. You hear from a company only if you ask to be connected to one.

Do you rank or rate window companies?

No. We publish no reviews, ratings, star scores or testimonials of our own, because we have not inspected anyone's work. A referral is not a certification. Check license and insurance yourself before you sign anything.

Can I rely on a national price range for my own house?

Treat it as a starting point for the conversation, not as a quote. Opening size, frame material, glazing, how many units, access, and what the installer finds when the old frame comes out all move the number, and labor rates vary widely by market.
